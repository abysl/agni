## Coaching

- **Master Yi - Tempered** — play him early and march him alone: every conquer or hold is 2 XP through Hunt, and at 6 XP he has Deflect and Ganking, so from then on move him from one battlefield straight to the next instead of going home.
- **Master Yi - Wuju Bladesman** — the legend gives a lone defender +2 might, so defend a battlefield with one unit, not two, and let En Garde make it +2 more.
- **Rengar - Trophy Hunter** — an Ambush unit: hold him in hand with a Body rune ready and play him as a reaction into a showdown you are losing, or onto a battlefield where only enemy units stand.
- **Lonely Poro** — a one-unit blocker that draws a card when it dies alone; never stack it with another unit at the same battlefield.
- **Scuttle Crab** — 0 might but it conquers and holds; its Deathknell reveals the opponent's hand and gives 1 XP, so throw it into a fight you expect to lose.
- **Rampage** — pay the extra Body rune only when the +2 might turns the trade into a kill; without it the spell is a plain fight between the two units.
- **Zhonya's Hourglass** — hide it for one rune at the battlefield Yi defends; it dies in his place and sends him home exhausted, which keeps his XP engine alive.
- **Star Spring** — playing a unit here lets another unit of yours move home for free: use it to rescue an exhausted Yi after he conquers.
