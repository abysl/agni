## Coaching

- **Kha'Zix - Voidreaver** — win combats to bank XP, then spend it: 1 XP and an exhaust buffs a unit, 2 XP moves an exhausted friendly unit from a battlefield home, out of a fight it would lose.
- **Kha'Zix - Mutating Horror** — an Ambush unit that wants a lone enemy: react him into a combat where the opponent has exactly one unit, for +2 might and 2 XP.
- **Rengar - Trophy Hunter** — an Ambush unit that can be played to a battlefield where only enemy units stand; keep a Body rune ready and surprise the defender.
- **Forbidding Waste** — a lone defender here has -2 might, so attack it with a single unit and win the exchange; never defend it alone yourself.
- **Void Assault** — moves one of yours and then one of theirs: drag an enemy unit onto a battlefield you attack so it fights on your terms, or pull their defender off a battlefield you want to conquer.
- **Hard Bargain** — counter a spell unless its controller pays 2 energy; play it when the opponent is tapped low on runes, and pay its Repeat cost when two spells are on the chain.
- **Stacked Deck** — look at the top three and keep the one card that wins the coming fight: a Punch First when a combat is near, a unit when the board is empty.
- **Fizz - Trickster** — replays a cheap spell from your trash on entry, so play him after a Punch First or Star-Crossed has been used.
- **Up from the Deep** — two Tentacle tokens now, and the spell comes back once from the trash through Flow for 3 energy; the second cast is banished, so plan it as the last play of a turn.
- **Onslaught** — +6 might in one fight and a Flow cast later from the trash for 4 energy: the deck's finisher, not its opener.
- **Vex - Apathetic** — park her at a battlefield and every unit the opponent plays there is stunned; she has Deflect, so removing her costs them a rune.
- **Tail-Cloaked Matriarch** — her Empower costs 2 energy and a Chaos rune and then replays a cheap unit from your trash for free: empower her once a good unit has died.
- **Shadow Order Disciple** — burning a card for +1 might is worth it only when that point kills or saves a unit; the burned card is gone.
