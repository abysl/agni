## Coaching

- **Nasus, Ascended** — Deflect 2 makes him expensive to target; his Empower costs 8 energy and then every conquer scores a point, so ramp the runes with the legend and empower him mid-game.
- **Nasus - Curator of the Sands** — a 7-energy play lets the legend exhaust to ready two runes: play the big card first, then the readied runes pay for a reaction.
- **Astral Heron** — while she stands at a battlefield your second card each turn costs 2 energy and two runes less: play a cheap first card, then the expensive one.
- **Ravenbloom Student** — every spell you play gives her +1 might for the turn; she is the unit that carries Bellows Breath and Discipline into a combat.
- **Bellows Breath** — deals 1 to up to three units at one location; pay the Repeat cost to deal 2 each and clear a battlefield of small units.
- **Thousand-Tailed Watcher** — enemy units drop 3 might for the turn when she is played; play her onto a battlefield you hold with Accelerate so she defends it ready.
- **Temporal Breach** — hide it for a rune, then react with it to banish an enemy unit; the owner replays it, so it is a tempo play against a unit with a play trigger the opponent does not want twice, or against a token, which is gone for good.
- **Time Warp** — an extra turn that banishes itself: cast it when the board is already yours, so the second beginning phase scores the holds.
- **Retreat** — return your own unit to hand in response to a kill or a lost combat; the channel it grants pays for replaying it.
- **Vilemaw's Lair** — units cannot move home from it, so attack it only with units you mean to leave there, and let the opponent strand theirs.
- **Rockfall Path** — nothing can be played here, only moved: it is the battlefield to conquer with one marching unit while the opponent's units are elsewhere.
