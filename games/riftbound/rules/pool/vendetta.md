# Vendetta (VEN)

Provenance: the Vendetta set, every base print ven-001 to ven-166 in collector order, from the Riftcodex catalog dump of 2026-09-10; alternate art, overnumbered and signature prints resolve to the base name through base_name, and where the dump carries two records of one print the later one (the record with a clean_name) is the row, which is how the legends read as the importer's canonical names (Nasus - Curator of the Sands, and Yordle, Kennen - Heart of the Tempest as the catalog spells it); the set's six basic runes ven-r01 to ven-r06 are the six runes Origins already lists under their own ids and the engine keys runes by the name suffix, so they carry no row here, and the six ven-sp prints (Kai'Sa, Survivor to Lux, Crownguard) are reprints the importer's ALIASES map to their Origins, Spiritforged and Proving Grounds names (Kai'Sa - Survivor to Lux - Crownguard), so they carry no row either; text from Riftcodex by riftbound id, with the attached line of each Equipment appended from the card image (the catalog omits it, the might badge last as +N :rb_might:) and Jagged Cutlass's Equip keyword bracketed as the card prints it (the catalog drops the brackets)

Scripted: partial

A set file, not a deck: it carries no deck block and no coaching. Every card line reads byte for byte as it does in the deck files that share it. The set prints no token faces of its own; the engine spawns tokens by name. Partial because 16 cards stay stubs — CARD is the generic constructor of its kind with the pool name and the printed keywords — since their whole text is one mechanic the engine does not have yet (each names its seam and carries an ignored test of the behaviour it wants; the M13 table in wiki/design/rules-engine.md lists them); the other 148 run on scripts of their own, two of them vanillas whose printed keywords are the whole script (Horns of the Dragon, Soulspinner), and the 18 cards shared with the deck files run on the deck files' scripts.
