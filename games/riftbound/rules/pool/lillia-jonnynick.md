## Coaching

- **Lillia - Bashful Bloom** — pay 4 energy and exhaust her to play a ready Sprite; every Temporary unit you control makes the next Sprite cheaper, so chain them in one turn.
- **Lillia - Fae Fawn** — play her with Accelerate for 1 energy and a Mind rune so she enters ready and attacks at once.
- **Sprite Fountain** — a Temporary gear that plays a ready Sprite when it lands and again through its Deathknell when it dies at your next beginning phase: two Sprites for one card, and each one makes the legend's Sprite cheaper.
- **Mask of Foresight** — a friendly unit that attacks or defends alone gets +1 might: send one unit to a battlefield, not two.
- **Brutalizer** — equip it for a Calm rune on the turn the unit fights: it is +3 might the turn it is attached and +1 after, so attach it before the march.
- **Smoke and Mirrors** — swaps two of your units at different places when one is Temporary; use it to pull a Sprite out of a lost fight and drop a real unit in.
- **Singularity** — up to two targets; count that the total is worth the cost before playing it into a single unit.
- **Janna - Savior** — a reaction unit playable straight to a battlefield you control: she heals your units there and sends one enemy unit home, so hold her for the combat the opponent has already committed to.
- **Stupefy** — -1 might on a unit for the turn and a card drawn; play it during a showdown so the point of might decides the fight.
