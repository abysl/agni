## Coaching

- **Lillia - Bashful Bloom** — pay 4 energy and exhaust her to play a ready Sprite; every Temporary unit you control makes the next Sprite cheaper, so chain Sprites in one turn and attack with them before they die at your next beginning phase.
- **Lillia - Fae Fawn** — play her with Accelerate for 1 energy and a Mind rune so she enters ready and can march the same turn.
- **Sprite Fountain** — a Temporary gear that plays a ready Sprite when it lands and again through its Deathknell when it dies at your next beginning phase; each Sprite makes the legend's next Sprite cheaper.
- **Smoke and Mirrors** — swaps two of your units at different places when one is Temporary: pull a Sprite out of a fight it loses and drop a real unit in.
- **Hwei - Brooding Painter** — moving him makes you discard; discard a spell to draw, a gear to ready runes, a unit for +3 might.
