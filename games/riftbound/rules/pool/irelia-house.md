## Coaching

- **Irelia - Blade Dancer** — whenever you choose a friendly unit with a spell or ability she may exhaust and pay a rune to ready it, and a conquer lets you pay 1 energy to ready her again: choose your own attacker with a buff and it attacks twice.
- **Boots of Swiftness** — equip them for a Chaos rune to the unit that attacks: Ganking lets it move from battlefield to battlefield without going home.
- **Edge of Night** — equip it before the fight, not after; the +2 might is what wins the exchange.
- **Zhonya's Hourglass** — hide it for one rune at the battlefield you defend; it dies in your unit's place and recalls the unit exhausted.
- **Switcheroo** — swaps the might of two units at one battlefield for the turn: play it in a combat where their big unit meets your small one.
- **Tideturner** — hide it for a rune and react with it later: on play it may trade places with a friendly unit elsewhere, pulling a threatened unit home and putting a fresh defender in.
- **Abandon** — counter a spell and send it back to its owner's hand; when the Predict then shows you the top card, recycle a card you do not want to draw and skip when it is good.
- **Scuttle Crab** — 0 might but it conquers and holds; its Deathknell reveals the opponent's hand and gives 1 XP, so send it where it will die.
