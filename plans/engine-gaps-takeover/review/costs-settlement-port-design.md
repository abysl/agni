Read-only plan against Play WIP based on e9235605, updated integration Costs checklist (86d04b44+), saved d0639152, and pinned Core 355–358. No builds or production edits. Paths below are relative to `games/riftbound-turns/src/` unless stated otherwise.

The later `cost-undo-seam-review.md` supplies the required SDK/engine extension
and supersedes blanket restoration of allocation cursors across committed
requests. Preserve exposed-ID high-water marks and accepted disclosure history;
never serialize private view overlays or recursively embed an entire GameBlob.

## Rule boundary: choice is not physical settlement

- 355.1.a declares whether an optional cost is accepted; mandatory costs require no yes/no prompt. This step emits no discard/kill/exhaust/return effects.
- 355.2/355.5 choose location and actual targets on the unchanged Board. 355.10.c excludes cost objects from targets; the 357.2.a example selects the kill victim in step 3. Do not append cost objects to execution targets or remembered callback suffixes.
- 356 chooses the concrete objects needed to calculate/pay the accepted cost, computes the complete cost and freezes the payment plan. 356.2.b.1 explicitly describes declaration, discount calculation, then discard payment at 357.2.
- 357.1 pays combined resources; 357.2 performs the non-standard cost(s), including genuine replacement processing; 358 rechecks targets, completed payment, outcome restrictions and timing before finalization. An operation merely parked for a replacement choice is not complete.
- **356.4.f.1 defines an optional cost as “paid” when the player chose it, even if its amount becomes zero.** Preserve the rules-facing `paid_additional()`/slot-3 meaning; use a separate settlement state to gate payment execution and finalization. Do not make every existing card consult a physical paid-object count for its “if you paid” branch.

## Declaration, selection and quotation

- Add `declare`, `candidates`, `select/validate`, `quote`, and `settle` boundaries in `engine/additional.rs`; keep descriptors in the card vocabulary. Functions/pointers are never serialized. The saved descriptor supports one additional-cost clause per card, sufficient for this recovery; do not claim it handles arbitrary future combined costs.
- Early admission must not reject a card because its undiscounted cost is unaffordable when an additional-cost choice can make it payable. Use the actual item and a valid candidate selection witness; avoid saved `play_item(Origin::Hand)` and generic affordability shortcuts. Recompute authoritative selection validity at admission/payment, not only in UI options.
- For ordinary effects, select the cost victim after the actual targets. Apply 357.3: exclude a victim whose payment deterministically invalidates a chosen target if another permissible payment exists. This is about the payment outcome, not simply “cost IDs must never equal target IDs”: a replacement can preserve the target. If no alternative exists, the exception permits that choice, but a failed 358 check still undoes the play.
- Stalking Wolf is a declaration dependency, not permission to kill early. Offer candidate destinations supported by a possible qualifying cost victim, then require a compatible victim before calculation. Alternatively gather a provisional cost-object choice first without effects, and validate the joint location/target/payment declaration before commitment. Prefer the first ordering for a single normal cost-selection stage. Its location query must use planned/frozen facts, never require the victim already to be in Trash. Test the victim remains present through location selection.
- `Count::AnyNumber` permits zero even when the candidate set is empty. Distinguish accepted zero objects from declining where the card's text makes that distinction. Do not silently truncate candidate count/indices at 255; apply the explicit finite limit policy until A6 removes that limit.
- Quote the complete item: actual origin, Limited price, optional costs, targets/Deflect, promised grants/Repeat, chosen modes, source qualifiers, resource pool and cost-selected facts. Feed the same item to the existing item-aware resource planner/affordability paths. Preserve A9 owned costs; do not add a second synthetic quote pipeline.
- Component discounts apply to their component; selection-dependent discounts are calculated before killing/discarding their source. Preserve the final whole-cost modifier order. `Price::Free` changes base cost only. `Price::Ignored` clears all physical costs, including mandatory non-standard costs and resource burn, but preserves optional-choice semantics and relevant grant metadata. Do not ask for a mandatory sacrifice merely to waive it afterward; a selected optional cost reduced/ignored to zero remains accepted without consuming an object.
- Do not pay optional rune additions twice: they join the existing total once and have no separate non-resource operation. Keep A10 multiple Flow/Repeat and A12 discount-order work explicitly separate.

## Completion and refusal prerequisite

Source finding: `engine/kill.rs::run_with` returns `Killed::Replaced` both after synchronous replacement and immediately after `park`. `park` creates an unrelated resolving trigger without a payment owner or completion result. Saved `additional::pay` treats either result as a paid victim; `settle_additional` ignores the resulting count. This cannot safely be ported unchanged.

The bounded seam is a typed cost operation, owned by the pending play, with an explicit result `Complete(receipt) | Pending(continuation_id) | Failed(reason)`. Persist a cost transaction under a new `GameBlob` key, e.g. `cost_payment = [owner_item, operation_cursor, waiting_continuation, frozen_quote, undo_state]`; no magic card stage or unrelated slot. The owner waits until the exact child operation returns a terminal receipt, including nested replacements. Duplicate answers cannot advance it twice; orphan owner/child links are refused deterministically. Finalization and the next cost operation are forbidden while waiting. Required operation results and links must be validated before consuming IDs or appending effects.

A cost wrapper alone does not repair synchronous replacement callbacks which themselves call kill and then continue. Any replacement callback reachable by this cost seam must already satisfy D1 completion, or be migrated within this bounded prerequisite. Do not infer completion by a missing prompt or by `is_parked(victim) == false`; a replacement can create further work on another object.

For the non-suspending payment path, take a checkpoint immediately before the first resource/self/non-standard payment mutation, apply the frozen plan, check 358, and either commit or restore plus cancel the declaration. Checkpoint restoration must preserve prior accepted work and restore the exact payment-start state: table/origin, blob, effects/events and collection cursor, death queue, spawned IDs, fault/win state, awaiting/picked/remembered data and relevant projection/defer flags. `chain::Checkpoint` currently saves only blob/effect/event lengths, spawned and a fault boolean; it clears awaiting and omits death/pick/remembered state. Reusing it unchanged is insufficient. Restore at the correct boundary relative to the already projected incoming Move, then use the existing declaration-origin cancellation path; do not replay that incoming Move twice.

For a cross-request payment, an in-memory checkpoint or vector length is not an undo record. `undo_state` must use the A5 serialized transaction/compensation contract, retaining the payment-start state and identifying the transaction's committed prefix, without undoing unrelated accepted work. A chooser must see already executed prefix effects and legitimate revealed information. Do not roll back an entire Rust callback to construct its prompt or replay that prefix after the answer. If later legality fails, compensation must restore paid resources/objects/counters and cost-generated queues exactly once; it cannot erase knowledge already disclosed. This is why the fourth cluster waits for the bounded A5/D1 prerequisite instead of assuming current checkpoints handle it.

Validate all picked objects/counts/filters and required destinations before mutating. Saved Discard can increment its paid count even when no Move occurred; saved Kill can consume an invalid/enemy pick if called directly. Use an all-or-failure settlement result, not partial-success counts followed by unconditional finalization. User cancellation is allowed before payment; rule-mandated undo/refusal after payment is a separate path, not a new cancel button.

Round-trip new fields and every retained schema fixture; replay representative accepted continuations under native and hardened wasm. No universal allocator/gas claim.
